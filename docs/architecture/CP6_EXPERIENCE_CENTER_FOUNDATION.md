# PactUS Pro CP-6 Experience Center Foundation

## Scope
CP-6 centralizes Help, Training, Support entry points, Improvements, Surveys, and What's New under one Experience Center.

## Training
The existing E-Learning experience is preserved as a centralized Learning Catalog with search, filters, resource types, tags, duration, author, ratings/views metadata, download capability, assignments, learning paths, completion, certification, and training-credit integration.

## Subscription and role awareness
Training visibility may depend on product/sub-module entitlement, permission, role, industry, implementation stage, and customer plan. Training visibility never grants product access.

## Existing E-Learning preservation
The eight sample resource topics supplied by the product owner are represented in the initial governed training catalog. Existing resource workflows must be inventoried before replacement.

## Help
Help/Knowledge Base is part of the Experience Center and should receive product/module context from the shell. Contextual Help buttons are shortcuts into the canonical Experience Center.

## Localization
All Experience Center navigation and metadata use localization keys. English and Spanish are required initial locales.
