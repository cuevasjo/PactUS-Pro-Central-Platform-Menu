# CP-10 Surveys & Customer Experience

Provides shared survey contracts for Technical Support and Sales & Implementation journeys.

Technical Support surveys may capture CSAT, timeliness, quality, knowledge, communication, resolution, ease, comments, and optional NPS.

Sales/Implementation surveys may be triggered after sales completion, go-live, training, or later customer-success checkpoints.

Low scores can be flagged for governed follow-up. Survey responses preserve organization, user, language, product/module, ticket, and implementation context.
