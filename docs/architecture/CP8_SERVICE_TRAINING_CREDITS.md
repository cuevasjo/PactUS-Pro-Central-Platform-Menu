# CP-8 Service Credits & Training Credits

Defines an auditable customer-credit wallet for SLA remedies, implementation remediation, customer-success intervention, promotions, and contractual allocations.

Supported remedy concepts include service/account credits, training hours, support extensions, and consultation.

Financial credits are never automatically issued solely because an SLA timer reaches zero. Qualification remains subject to contractual rules, exclusions, caps, and review.

Training-hour balances support expiration and consumption tracking. CP-12 will expose administration and CP-14/15 will validate production policy behavior.

Status: implementation foundation complete; certification pending.
